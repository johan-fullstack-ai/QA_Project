## Actions based on test results
### In case of variation between answers:
The temperature setting for the model can be lowered depending on the variance shown in the test. Seeding the model also reduces variation. It is also possible to add to the prompt for text tests:
Answer in one sentence and do not refer to previous responses nor corrections, but this is mainly needed for smaller local models.

### Images and semantic deviations:
For images and semantic deviations, it is mainly important to keep the images as clean as possible and lower the temperature of the model.
